Where the next art pass should go

  1. Depth-aware labels

    Names can still show through props. The separate WebGPU text layer needs scene-depth occlusion.

  2. More distinct creature bodies

    Flyer and biped silhouettes are too close, especially when a hat hides the propeller.

  3. More personality in plain fish

    Koi, Guppy and Puffer stand out. Chub and Bloop still need stronger individual character.

  4. The tiny joins

    Small SDF irregularities remain visible at close inspection. Some secondary foliage is still clumpy.
